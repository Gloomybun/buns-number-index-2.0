Why doesn't the demon realm debuff or adaptation change anything?

The way skills work is they deal damage based on a % of your attack stat. If you have 100 physical attack and a skill does 500% physical attack damage, you will do 500 damage. Environmental debuffs decrease your actual attack stat. 30% environmental debuff means that your attack power becomes 70, but the skill damage stays 500%. Right now, this calculator only calculates % damage rather than a numerical value, and the % remains the same regardless of environmental debuffs. The option to input environmental debuffs is here for my testing.
