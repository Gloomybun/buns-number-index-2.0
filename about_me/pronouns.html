My comfort level with different pronouns changes regularly, based on the setting, who's using them, who's around, my mood, and a million other factors that are difficult to pinpoint.

It's hard for me to say exactly what's going to feel 'right' in any given moment, so to speak, so if you ever need to refer to me by anything, just pick a pronoun!

If I am uncomfortable with the one you've chosen, I will let you know.

I won't hold anything against you.

If you need to refer to me in a situation where I am not there to see or correct you, I could not care less what you use.


I am fine with most gendered terms (ie. "girl", "man", "dude" etc.) when they're used as interjections or vocatives.

When refering to me directly, I prefer gender neutral or male terms of adress (ie. "son" or "kid" as opposed to "daughter")

UNLESS

You get creative with it!

I enjoy being referred to by titles of nobility, regardless of their gender connotation.

This very much is related to my gender but that's much too long and much too personal of a tangent to put on this page.

I like titles especially when they're used casually as an interjectives or vocatives (ie. the 'king' in "don't worry about it king").

Please, call me marchioness or count palatine or earl.

I cannot even begin to describe the joy that brings me.
